# All-quest preparation checklist — local LostCity / 2004scape version

Prepared 23 October 2026. Local `server.json` selects **revision 245.2**, identified by `server/start.ts` as **13 July 2004**. Scope: **57 quests in the bundled game quest journal, plus the two wiki miniquests (Barcrawl and Mage Arena)**. This is a checklist for a character completing the whole local quest set from scratch, not an audit of any particular bot’s bank or completion status.

The requested “wiki skill” was not found in the installed skill locations or repo-local skills. This report uses the repository’s `wiki/quests/` directly, checked against game scripts and relevant promoted DVR preparation notes. The imported modern OSRS helper library is not used to define this game’s quest set. The currently deployed remote server build has not been fingerprinted; local scripts are the version boundary for this report.

## How to use this checklist

- **Stock before route** includes consumables and reusable tools. It is a convenient preparation list, not a claim that every item is required in inventory to start the quest.
- **Obtain during quest / conditions** identifies quest-stage items, alternatives, prerequisite quests and route-dependent supplies. Quest rewards used later should be retained instead of purchased twice.
- An unnumbered tool means one reusable item. Ordinary materials/hand-ins should be unnoted unless a specific route says otherwise. Buffer quantities are explicitly labeled; misses, burns, failures, repeated travel and combat prevent a universal exact consumable total.
- Food, armour, weapons, prayer/antipoison potions and combat runes must match the character and selected route. Teleports are convenience supplies unless a quest step explicitly requires a spell.

## Shared bank kit

Keep one hammer, knife/slashing tool, spade, chisel, pestle and mortar, needle, tinderbox, usable pickaxe, **rune axe**, machete, fishing rod, shears, ring mould, necklace mould and silver-sickle mould. Keep leather gloves, leather boots, white apron, empty pot, bucket and jug; stock thread, ropes, light sources and waterskins. Fishing rod may be converted into the Heroes’ oily rod, so do Fishing Contest first or keep a second rod. Four buckets make Plague City faster, but one refillable bucket works.

Retain **ghostspeak amulet, Excalibur, climbing boots, anti-dragon shield (early item 1540), dusty key, ice gloves, boots of lightness and gas mask** as acquired. Gang-specific black armour is listed under Heroes’ Quest. Do not treat a shield’s early display name “Dragonfire shield” as the modern visage shield.

## Important corrections to existing notes

1. Dragon Slayer’s wizard’s mind bomb is a door ingredient even with high Magic. Buy-map and Telegrab are alternative routes.
2. Druidic Ritual requires four specific raw meat types, one each.
3. Observatory’s actual materials are 3 planks, 1 bronze bar and 1 molten glass.
4. Family Crest also needs all five cooked fish and perfect-gold jewellery; normal gold bars do not replace perfect gold.
5. Legends needs all seven gems, rune axe, lockpick, rune-door runes and orb-spell supplies. Its journal lists **52 Mining**, not the wiki’s 50. The bull roarer is obtained from a forester, not crafted from achey logs and rope.
6. Priest in Peril’s saved prep note says “50 rune essence noted”, but the bundled Drezel script explicitly rejects notes. Use unnoted essence at hand-in. This conflict is not evidence that noted essence works on the current remote server.
7. Watchtower’s wiki summary omits gold bar, dragon bones, 20 coins, vial of water and pickaxe.
8. Prince Ali’s wiki summary omits pink skirt. Shilo’s wiki summary omits several route tools and bones, and incorrectly suggests needle/thread for its beads recipe.
9. Mage Arena’s first staff is free; the 80,000-coin extra-staff purchase is not a completion requirement.
